Research

Undergraduate thesis, NUML

Effect of Cyberslacking on Attention Control and Rational Thinking Among Employees in Workplace Settings

Cyberslacking is the use of the internet for non-work purposes during working hours. My thesis examined how it relates to employees’ attention control and to rational decision-making in the workplace.

The study used a quantitative design, from data collection through to statistical analysis and evidence-based reporting.
